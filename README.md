\# AI Prompt Manager



\*\*Save AI prompts locally, organize them with folders \& variables, and copy them with a single tap.\*\*



!\[Platform](https://shields.io)

!\[License](https://shields.io)

!\[Language](https://shields.io)



\## ✨ Features



\* \*\*Local Storage:\*\* Keep all your favorite AI prompts securely on your device – fast, offline-ready, and private.

\* \*\*Folder Organization:\*\* Structure your prompt library cleanly into custom folders and categories.

\* \*\*Dynamic Variables:\*\* Create reusable prompt templates with placeholders/variables that you can fill in right before copying.

\* \*\*One-Tap Copy:\*\* Quickly copy finalized prompts to your clipboard to paste them into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or any other AI tool.

\* \*\*Bilingual Interface:\*\* Available in English and German.
